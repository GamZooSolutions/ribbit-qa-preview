Using Your Friend’s Credit Card for Your Purchase –

Is It Considered Ribbit If He Gets the Points?

Shalom, the new Ribbit Q&A flyer is here. The printable PDF is attached to this email.

RIBBIT INSIGHT
Points are a reward from the card company for the swipe, not a gain from the borrower. Nothing extra passes between friends, so no ribbit.
